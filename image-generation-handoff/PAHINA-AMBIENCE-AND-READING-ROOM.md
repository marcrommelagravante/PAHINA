## 1. Core Spatial Concept: One Single Open Café Room

PAHINA is **one single open café room**—there is **no separate back room or closed-off library hall**. The cashier & espresso bar, the **two 5-layer bookshelves**, and the **many sets of tables, chairs, and power outlets** all share the same open, welcoming floorplan (modeled directly on `10-ref-single-room-cashier-layout.jpg`).

1. **Right Side — Cashier, Pastry Display & Espresso Bar Counter:**
   - A long architectural counter running along the right side of the room (matching the counter geometry in `10-ref-single-room-cashier-layout.jpg`).
   - Built with **vertical warm natural oak wood slats** along the front face and a **soft warm under-counter LED glow** along the base, topped with a clean stone/matte countertop, espresso machines, pour-over gear, register/cashier station, and a glass display case at the far end.
   - Above the back bar hangs a clean, minimal **overhead menu board frame** (with non-legible/abstract editorial layout lines—no gibberish text).
2. **Back-Left Feature Wall Next to the Cashier — Two 5-Layer Bookshelves:**
   - Directly beside the far end of the cashier/display counter (the exact wall position of the 5-tier shelf in `10-ref-single-room-cashier-layout.jpg`) sit **two matching 5-layer (5-tier) warm oak wooden bookshelves** side by side.
   - Because the two 5-layer bookshelves sit right next to the cashier counter facing the main walkway and seating area, guests order their coffee or food at the register, step right beside the counter to pick a book from the 5 horizontal tiers, and return it to the same counter-side collection zone before leaving.
3. **Left Side & Open Floor — Many Sets of Tables, Chairs & Power Outlets:**
   - Across a wide, comfortable central walkway from the cashier counter sits the main reading and dining zone (matching the left side of `10-ref-single-room-cashier-layout.jpg`).
   - **Wall Banquette Row:** A long cushioned oatmeal-linen/warm-tan wall banquette bench paired with a continuous row of warm oak tables and matching wooden lounge chairs opposite each table.
   - **Additional Floor Table Sets:** Extra 2-person and 4-person warm oak table-and-chair sets arranged neatly across the open floor so solo readers, students, and small groups have plenty of places to sit.
   - **Accessible Power Outlets:** Clean flush architectural duplex power + USB outlets built along the lower pastel blue-grey wall wainscoting and banquette base beside every table set so guests can plug in laptops, tablets, or phones for multi-hour reading and study stays.

## 2. Ambience, Color Palette & Lighting Direction

We take the **exact single-room architecture and counter/seating layout of `10-ref-single-room-cashier-layout.jpg`** and dress it in **PAHINA's signature daylight editorial palette** so every photo harmonizes with the website UI:

| Element | PAHINA Visual Specification |
| --- | --- |
| **Walls & Ceiling** | Muted pastel **Powder Blue** (`#B9D0E0`) paneled feature walls (behind the two 5-layer bookshelves and seating wall) paired with warm **Paper White** (`#F4F1EA`) upper walls/ceiling and subtle **Deep Ink Grey** (`#41474D`) trim. |
| **Cashier Counter** | Vertical **warm natural oak wood slats** with soft warm linear under-counter lighting, clean stone top, glass display case, espresso machine, and minimalist overhead menu board frame. |
| **The Bookshelves** | **Two side-by-side 5-layer (5-tier) warm oak bookshelves** mounted/recessed on the pastel Powder Blue wall right next to the cashier counter, filled with muted cloth-bound books across 6 genres. |
| **Tables, Chairs & Outlets** | **Many sets of warm oak tables and cushioned oatmeal-linen/oak chairs** (long wall banquette row + freestanding table sets) with **visible flush wall & banquette power outlets** at table/baseboard level. |
| **Lighting & Mood** | Soft natural daylight entering from tall windows blended with warm architectural café track lighting and the soft under-counter glow—calm, welcoming, unhurried, and practical for hours of reading or study. |
